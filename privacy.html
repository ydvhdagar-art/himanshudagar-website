Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to himanshudagar.in. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information you share while visiting this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our "Let's Talk" contact form, we only collect the name, optional email address, and message you voluntarily provide so we can respond to your greeting or inquiry. When you use the Greeting Card Maker, your name is processed directly in your browser and is not stored on any server.

2. How We Use Information

The details you submit via the contact form are used strictly to read and reply to your messages. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. External Links

Our website contains links to external platforms such as WhatsApp, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn. Once you leave our site, their respective privacy policies apply.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out through our Let's Talk page.
